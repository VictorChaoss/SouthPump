ABOUT

South Pump is what happens when crypto degens and South Park energy collide at 3am on a bull run.

What is South Pump?

South Pump ($SOUTHPUMP) is a parody memecoin living on Solana. It has no utility, no roadmap, and no apologies. It is a love letter to the chaotic, unhinged world of crypto degenerate culture — told through the lens of construction paper cutout animation.

What is South Pump+?

South Pump+ is the world's first streaming platform for crypto parody content. Fake episodes, real vibes. It's Netflix for degens. Think documentaries about SBF's prison diet, dramas about god candles, and comedies about going to the moon. Except we never actually make it.

Why does this exist?

Because the crypto space takes itself too seriously. We decided to build something that doesn't. South Pump is here to make you laugh, lose money with dignity, and maybe — just maybe — turn a bag along the way.

The Team

A small crew of degens, builders, and people who probably should be sleeping right now. Based everywhere. Anonymous mostly. Committed fully.
